---
name: macro-selection
description: >-
  Choose which modules of a large design to harden as macros by their interfaces in space
  (pins per micron of perimeter, from the RTL) and in time (registered, shallow boundary
  crossings, from a hierarchical synthesis), then plan the parent floorplan those
  interfaces dictate: pin sides, aspect ratios, channels, a logic region, the die. Use when
  a hierarchical flow's global route will not converge, when a macro's pin side is a wall,
  or before hardening anything on a design that needs breaking up.
---
